My Dearest Wifeyy,

I was wrong.

I was mad because I was scared. Scared of losing you. Scared of being alone. And instead of telling you that, I let my anger speak. I pushed you when I should have held you closer.

You didn't deserve that. You've given me nothing but love for two years. Your smile lights up my darkest days. Your heart is the safest place I've ever known. Your laugh is my favorite sound in the entire world.

Please don't go. Please give me the chance to be better. To love you better. To be the hubby you deserve.

You are my wifeyy. You always have been. You always will be.

I love you more than any words can ever say. And I'll spend the rest of my life proving it.

Forever yours,
Hubby
